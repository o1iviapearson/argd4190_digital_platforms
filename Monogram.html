01 / VISUAL IDENTITY / GRAPHIC SYSTEMS

PERSONAL MONOGRAM

An exploration of personal identity through typography and letterforms.

PROJECT

Personal Monogram

COURSE

Graphic Systems

DISCIPLINE

Visual Identity / Typography

01 / OVERVIEW

This project challenged me to create a personal monogram using the letters of my name. The goal was to transform individual letterforms into a cohesive visual identity.

02 / CONCEPT

The monogram explores the relationship between positive and negative space, overlapping forms, and typographic structure.

The black-and-white palette emphasizes the form itself, allowing the composition and relationships between the letters to become the focus of the design.

03 / PROCESS

The design process centered on experimenting with letterforms, their proportions, and how they could interact to form a recognizable mark.

This section can be expanded with sketches, iterations, and images documenting the development of the final monogram.

04 / FINAL OUTCOME

The final monogram combines my initials into a unified graphic mark that can be applied across different formats and materials.
